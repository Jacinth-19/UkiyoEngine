Ukiyo Engine

A Vulkan engine and world editor for stylised anime characters and worlds.

Ukiyo is a small engine built around a toon and anime pipeline: the Custom Anime tonemap, outlines, a face SDF, and a shader graph that writes .ukshader files. Terrain, foliage and water are part of the editor, with physics through Jolt, navigation through Recast and Detour, Lua scripting, particles, audio through miniaudio, and UDP networking with reliable sessions.

Projects are folders. This hub creates one from a template, records it in ukiyo.project.json, and hands it to the editor with --project. Nothing is installed and nothing is downloaded.

Anime pipeline

Toon shading, outlines, face SDF and a node-based shader graph.

World building

Terrain with layers and foliage, water with Gerstner waves, prefabs.

Game systems

Jolt physics, Recast navigation, Lua scripts, particles and audio.

Networking

UDP client and server sessions with reliable, ordered messages.
